Data Preprocessing Pipeline

Normalization is grouped into three main goals:

  • Sample normalization: adjustment for systematic differences among samples.
  • Data transformation: mathematical transformation to handle skewed distributions.
  • Batch correction: adjustment for batch effects to ensure comparability across different experimental runs.
  • Data scaling: adjusts each variable based on its dispersion.
